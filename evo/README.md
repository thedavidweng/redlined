# Redlined · Evo

Evo is a car-sharing service in Vancouver: members find a Prius on a map in the iOS app and book it for the minute, the hour or the day.

There are no redlines yet. This folder holds the replica in `mock/` (the map home, the selected car with its sheet, and the sheet expanded to the full-screen card with fees and rates). The visuals are calibrated by eye against reference screenshots, not 1:1 (see `mock/README.md`).
