# Range in kilometres

**Problem** The scooter sheet quotes the price in CAD but the range in miles ("13 mi range"). Canada uses the metric system, so riders have to convert.

**Change** The range shows in kilometres, converted from the same figure ("21 km range"). Nothing else on the sheet changes.

**Caption**

> The price on this sheet is in Canadian dollars, but the range is in miles. Canada measures in kilometres, so 13 mi becomes 21 km. Same scooter, same sheet, one less sum to do before you ride.
